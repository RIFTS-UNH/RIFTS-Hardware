# Future Work & Retrospective

## Future Work

Although the enclosure successfully met its design objectives, several opportunities remain for future refinement.

Potential improvements include:

- Slotted removable cover for faster servicing.
- Improved RFSoC mounting strategy to simplify installation.
- Evaluation of conductive EMI gaskets for removable panel interfaces.
- Additional quantitative shielding effectiveness measurements.
- Continued refinement of airflow management accessories.
- Integration of future RF hardware platforms using the established enclosure architecture.

---

## Retrospective

The RFSoC 4x2 enclosure represents the culmination of approximately eighteen months of mechanical design work completed as part of the RIFTS project. While the finished enclosure successfully met its design objectives, the development process provided several valuable engineering lessons.

One of the most significant was balancing electromagnetic performance with maintainability. Dense fastener spacing around the removable cover improved shielding effectiveness by minimizing seam apertures, but routine access required removing twenty-eight screws. Future revisions could incorporate slotted mounting features that preserve shielding while greatly simplifying maintenance.

The project also highlighted the importance of designing for assembly. Positioning the RFSoC 4x2 development board to maintain external connector accessibility resulted in limited tool access during installation, emphasizing the need to consider assembly procedures alongside mechanical layout early in the design process.

Perhaps the greatest lesson was the value of iterative engineering. The enclosure evolved through multiple design revisions as manufacturing, integration, and testing revealed new constraints. These iterations ultimately produced a more robust design and reinforced that successful engineering is achieved through continual refinement rather than a perfect first design.
